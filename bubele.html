I build software that checks paperwork, so people don't pay what they don't owe. Right now that means ocean demurrage and detention invoices for US importers.

Why I started FreeTime Check

I've worked in IT for more than 12 years. I spent four years servicing printers and copiers for businesses, then four years in university ICT support. Since 2022 I've run library systems at a large South African university, where I led the move of more than 8,200 research records to a new repository. I have a BTech in IT and a postgraduate diploma in library and information studies, and I'm finishing a Master of IT.

Most of that work comes down to checking records carefully against the rules. FreeTime Check does the same for demurrage invoices.

In 2024 the Federal Maritime Commission's billing rule (46 CFR 541) gave US importers clear rights: a demurrage or detention invoice must show specific details and arrive on time, or it may not be payable. Checking every line takes time most logistics teams don't have. So I built a tool to do the checking, and I handle every decision myself.
